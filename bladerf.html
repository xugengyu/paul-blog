BladeRF 2.0 micro

The Nuand BladeRF 2.0 micro is a 2x2 MIMO software-defined radio featuring a Cyclone V FPGA and an RF range from 47 MHz to 6 GHz. It serves as my primary workbench for advanced RF analysis, custom HDL development, and MIMO experimentation.

Hardware Specifications

RF Range
47 MHz - 6.0 GHz
MIMO Support
2x2 Channels
Sample Rate
Up to 61.44 MSPS
FPGA
Altera Cyclone V
