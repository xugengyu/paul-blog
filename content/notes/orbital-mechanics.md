Kepler's laws govern satellite motion. Key orbits: LEO (200–2 000 km, 90–120 min period), MEO (2 000–35 786 km), and GEO (35 786 km, 24 h period, 0° inclination).

Orbital velocity at LEO is ~7.8 km/s; at GEO it's ~3.1 km/s. ΔV budgets, eclipse durations, and radiation environment all depend on altitude.
