The cornerstone of circuit analysis: <code>V = I × R</code>. Voltage across a resistor equals the current through it multiplied by its resistance.

In AC circuits this generalises to <code>V = I × Z</code>, where Z is complex impedance. Ohm's Law is the starting point for virtually every RF and power calculation you'll ever do.
