---

<strong>KCL</strong> — the sum of currents entering a node is zero. <strong>KVL</strong> — the sum of voltages around any closed loop is zero.

These two laws, combined with Ohm's Law, let you solve arbitrarily complex linear networks. They underpin SPICE simulation and every nodal analysis algorithm.
