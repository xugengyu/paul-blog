An antenna converts guided electromagnetic waves into free-space radiation (and vice versa). Key parameters: gain (dBi), beam-width, polarisation, bandwidth, and radiation efficiency.

Satellite ground stations commonly use parabolic reflectors (high gain, narrow beam), while spacecraft may use horn, patch array, or phased-array antennas depending on coverage requirements.
