Noise figure (NF) quantifies how much a component degrades SNR: <code>NF = SNR_in / SNR_out</code> (linear) or in dB. Friis's formula shows that the first stage dominates system NF.

A satellite LNB with NF = 0.7 dB corresponds to a noise temperature of ~50 K — critical for closing links with spacecraft EIRP as low as 40 dBW.
