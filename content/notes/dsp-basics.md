Digital signal processing replaces analog filters, mixers, and demodulators with mathematical operations on sampled data. Core concepts: sampling theorem (Nyquist), DFT/FFT, FIR & IIR filtering, and correlation.

In software-defined radios (SDRs), the entire receive chain from IF down-conversion onward is implemented in an FPGA or CPU, enabling flexible waveform support and rapid prototyping.
