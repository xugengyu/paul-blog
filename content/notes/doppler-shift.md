A LEO satellite at 550 km altitude moves at ~7.6 km/s relative to the ground, causing a Doppler shift of ±Δf/f ≈ ±v/c ≈ ±25 ppm.

At S-band (2.2 GHz) that's ±55 kHz — well within typical receiver AFC range. At Ka-band it can exceed ±600 kHz, requiring fast carrier tracking loops.
