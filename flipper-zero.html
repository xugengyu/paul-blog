Flipper Zero

The Flipper Zero is a portable multi-tool for pentesters and geeks in a toy-like body. It loves researching Sub-GHz radio protocols, RFID, NFC, infrared, and hardware debugging.

Capabilities

Sub-GHz
300-928 MHz (CC1101)
RFID & NFC
125kHz & 13.56MHz
Infrared

GPIO Headers
For custom modules
